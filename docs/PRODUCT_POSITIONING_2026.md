# Cozinha 360 OS — product positioning 2026

## Core promise

**Saiba quanto cobrar, quanto produzir e o que corrigir antes de crescer.**

Cozinha 360 OS is not positioned as a broad restaurant ERP. It is a beginner-first operating system for home-based delivery and small dark kitchens.

## Buyer

- home-based food business
- confectionery and bakery microbusiness
- delivery-first kitchen
- small dark kitchen running one or more virtual brands

## Jobs to be done

1. Capture every order into one operating queue.
2. Know whether an order still contributes after recipe and channel costs.
3. Share ingredient inventory across virtual brands.
4. Know what to prepare without guessing.
5. Protect service capacity before increasing ad spend.
6. Build a consented first-party customer base for direct-channel repeat purchase.

## Product doctrine

- Contribution before revenue vanity.
- Capacity before traffic.
- Canonical internal order model before marketplace integrations.
- First-party data by consent, never scraping restricted marketplace customer data.
- Official provider APIs where access is available; integrations degrade gracefully when unavailable.
- Fiscal, payroll and statutory modules are provider/jurisdiction-specific and never marketed as universal compliance.

## North-star decision

Every important screen should help answer one of six questions:

1. Is this order profitable?
2. How much should I charge here?
3. Did my ingredient cost change enough to review price?
4. How many orders can I safely accept/produce?
5. Who can I invite back with consent?
6. What is the single next action today?
